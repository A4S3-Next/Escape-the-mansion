How to play

"Take turns rolling the die, then choose a direction — up, down, left, or right — to move that many rooms through the mansion. The halls loop back on themselves at each edge, so every direction is always open. Three rooms — secret, and different every game — lead outside. Reach any one of them first and you've escaped. Traps litter some rooms, power-ups wait in others."

Traps

Trapdoor

Move back 3 spaces

Ghost Attack

Lose your next turn

Spider Nest

Move back 2 spaces

Locked door

Need a skeleton key or lose your next turn

Collapsing Staircase

Go back a room

Haunted Mirror

Move back 2 spaces

Bat Swarm

Roll a 4, 5, or 6 to escape or lose a turn

Cursed Room

Roll to determine the penalty

Falling Chandelier

Move back 1 space and lose a turn

Fake Exit

Move back 4 spaces

Power-ups

Skeleton Key

Unlock any locked door without losing a turn.

Flashlight

Ignore one Ghost Attack.

Holy Water

Cancel one curse or ghost attack.

Map

Reveal what lies in each room next to you.

Sprint Boots

Move an extra 3 spaces on your next roll.

Protective Charm

Blocks the very next trap that affects you.

Extra Life

Ignore a trap that would cost you a turn.

Teleport Crystal

Move to any room you've already visited.

Time Rewind

Undo the last trap that affected you.

Lucky Coin

Reroll the dice once on your turn.
